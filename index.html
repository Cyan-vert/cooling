What is cooling and types of cooling and how it works.

Cooling

Cooling is a sytem of fans, heatsinks and special liquids that remove heat from computers hardware like CPU, and GPU to keep them from overheating and triggering that saftey measure call thermal throttling which is when the hardware gets to hoot and the performance slows down to prevent permanent damage. A good cooling keeps the parts safe at good temperatures so they can run at full speed.

Types of Cooling

Air cooling

Air cooling is a cost effective and reliable gaming computer cooling system for the CPU. It uses fans and heatsinks and the sytem efficiently dissipate thermal enegry, making them an excellent choice for new people to PC building and thhose who have a budjet.

How it works

Air cooling works by tranferring heat away from a hot componet and releasing it into the surrounding air by using heatsink (which are made of copper or alumiinum for high heat conductivity) it is attached to the hot componet like cpu and gpu. Heat flows out of the componets and into the heat sink base . Then heat pipes transfer the thermal energy up into thin metal fins for easier surface for heat to be dumped at, then an electric fan blows the air acroos the metal fins forcing the thermal enery to transfer from the hot metal into air, then the hot air is pushed out by case fans letting cooler air take its place.

Benefits for air cooling

  1. It is cost effectiveness.
  2. It is highly reliable and simple.
  3. It is easy to install.

Downside for air cooling

  1. Noise under heavy loads.
  2. It accumualte dust and requires rountine cleaning.
  3. It has a very bulky size.

Liquid cooling

Liquid cooling heat management sytem for computers that uses water based coolant insted of air to pull heat away for hot prts like CPU and GPU, and it is a more expensive than air cooling.

How it works

Liquid cooling works by a metal block sit on top of the CPU or GPU with thermal paste(is a heat-conducting gel applied between a computer processor and its cooler to fill in microscopic air gaps and ensure efficient heat transfer.) in between and as the the componets generate heat, thermal energy conduts through the metal block into tiny internal channels inside the block and absorbs the thermal energy via conduction then a mechanical pump pushes the heated liquid through flexible or hard tubes and this forcves the warm coolant away from the hot components ttoward the edge of the case then the hot liquid enters the radiator, which is a nwtwork of thin metal fins and tubes with a large surface area then thermal energy moves from the liquid into the metal fins then attached fans blow the air across the metal fins pushing the heat out of the computer and into the surrounding air then the cooled liquid returns to the water block on CPU or GPU and the cycle continues as long as the computer is turned on.

Benefits for liquid cooling

  1. Better Heat Transfer.
  2. Thermal Buffering.
  3. Prevention of Thermal Throttling.
  4. Quieter Operation.
  5. Better for Gaming.

Downside for liquid cooling

  1. It is more expensive than air cooling.
  2. It is more complex to install and maintain.
  3. It has a risk of leaks and potential damage to components.
  4. Shorter Lifespan.

Types of Liquid Cooling

  1. All in One (AIO) / Closed-Loop Coolers: Pre-assembled, factory-sealed, and low maintenance units designed mainly for CPUs.
  2. Custom Loops: Hand-assembled systems offering more control and customization options that can both cool the CPU and GPU.
  3. Hybrid Cooling: Combines air and liquid cooling, often using liquid for the CPU and air for the GPU.
